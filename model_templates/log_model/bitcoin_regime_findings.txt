RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. If slope is decreasing over time, consider using a more recent
   time period for the growth model (e.g., post-2020 data)

   be appropriate - consider piecewise or trend+cycle models
3. Use the identified regime shift dates as breakpoints for
   piecewise models if significant shifts are detected

   then test predictions on 2021-2025 data
